# Explore: Threadbare -- Full Curriculum (English)

Extracted 4 August 2026 from the English source decks in Google Drive. Thirteen standalone workshops across five disciplines.

**Program shape:** this is NOT a cohort. Each of the 13 workshops is a self-contained one-hour session that a learner can attend on its own, in any combination. The platform should present these as "join a workshop" with a date, not as a cohort date range.

**Common structure:** every workshop follows the same arc -- driving question, an "What is Open Source?" contribution framing, play a *Threadbare* quest, reflect, a 20 to 40 minute make activity, share using the Compliment / Coach / Encourage feedback protocol, a Self-Check with a tinyurl, then two career-connection slides (in the industry / in other industries). Each names a concrete deliverable that becomes a community contribution.

**Fidelity note:** on-slide text is verbatim. Speaker notes came through for the two Go-To-Market decks but not for the other eleven, and most hyperlink targets are lost by the extraction (link labels are preserved). Every gap is flagged inline rather than filled in.

See sessions.md for the full workshop-by-workshop teaching content.
